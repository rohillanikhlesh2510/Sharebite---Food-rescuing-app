## 🚨 The Problem We're Solving

### India's Twin Crisis

India faces two of the world's most pressing humanitarian problems — **simultaneously**:

| Crisis | Scale |
|--------|-------|
| 🗑️ Food wasted every year in India | **68.8 million tonnes** |
| 😔 People going to bed hungry in India | **194 million** |
| 🏆 India's rank in Global Hunger Index 2023 | **111 out of 125** |
| 💰 Annual economic loss from food waste | **₹92,000 crore (\$11 billion)** |
| 🌍 India's share of global food waste | **~40% of produced food** |

> These two crises exist **side by side**. The food to feed every hungry person in India is being thrown away every single day — in restaurants, hotels, weddings, households, and events.

**Sharebite exists to close this gap.**

### The Numbers Are Staggering

| Statistic | Data |

### Why It Hasn't Been Fixed (Until Now)

The problem persists because there was **no real-time bridge** between:
- Surplus food (which spoils within hours)
- Nearby NGOs and communities (who need it right now)
- Riders and volunteers (who can transport it)

Sharebite builds that bridge — digitally, in real time, across India.

## 📐 Impact Metrics Framework

Sharebite tracks impact across **4 dimensions**:

### Metric Definitions

| Metric | How We Calculate | Unit |
|--------|-----------------|------|
| **Meals Saved** | 1 serving = 1 meal = approx. 350g of food | meals |
| **Food Rescued** | Servings × 350g (average meal weight) | kg |
| **CO₂ Prevented** | 1 kg food waste = 2.5 kg CO₂ equivalent (IPCC) | kg CO₂e |
| **Water Saved** | 1 kg food = avg. 1,000 litres virtual water (FAO) | litres |
| **People Reached** | Unique beneficiaries who received food | people |
| **Economic Value** | Meals rescued × ₹80 (avg. meal cost in India) | INR |
| **Land Equivalent** | Based on avg. agricultural land per kg food type | m² |

## 🌱 Environmental Impact

### Food Waste & Climate Change

Food waste is the **third largest source of greenhouse gas emissions** globally. When food rots in landfills, it releases **methane** — a gas 25× more potent than CO₂.

**Every meal Sharebite rescues prevents:**

| Resource | Saved per Meal (350g) |
|----------|----------------------|
| 🌫️ CO₂ equivalent | 0.875 kg CO₂e |
| 💧 Water | 350 litres virtual water |
| 🌾 Land | ~0.5 m² agricultural land equivalent |
| ⚡ Energy | ~0.4 kWh (production + transport) |

### Impact Calculation Example

**If Sharebite rescues 10,000 meals per month:**

| Environmental Saving | Amount |
|---------------------|--------|
| 🌫️ CO₂ prevented | **8,750 kg CO₂e / month** |
| 💧 Virtual water saved | **3,500,000 litres / month** |
| 🌾 Agricultural land equivalent | **5,000 m² / month** |
| ♻️ Landfill waste diverted | **3,500 kg / month** |

> **8,750 kg CO₂ = equivalent to taking 4 cars off the road for a year**

### India's Food System Environmental Cost

| Factor | Data |
|--------|------|
| Agriculture's share of India's GHG emissions | **~18%** |
| Food wasted emits (India, annual) | **~700 million tonnes CO₂e** |
| Potential CO₂ reduction if 50% food waste eliminated | **~350 million tonnes CO₂e** |
| India's total annual CO₂ emissions | **~2.9 billion tonnes** |

**Every meal Sharebite rescues is a small but real vote for the planet.**

## 📖 Impact Stories

### 🍽️ Story 1 — The Wedding That Fed 300 Families

> *A wedding in Delhi had 500 guests but prepared food for 700 — a common practice in India. Post-event, the event manager listed 200 kg of surplus biryani, dal, and sweets on Sharebite at 10 PM. By 11 PM, 3 NGOs had claimed the food. By midnight, 2 riders had completed deliveries. By 7 AM, 300 families in JJ Colony had eaten a hot meal.*
>
> **Without Sharebite:** 200 kg → municipal garbage bin
> **With Sharebite:** 200 kg → 600 meals → 300 families fed

---

### 🥗 Story 2 — The Restaurant That Became a Hero

> *A restaurant owner in Bangalore was throwing away 15–20 meal boxes every night after closing time. After joining Sharebite, the same food now goes to a nearby shelter home every evening. In 6 months, the restaurant has donated 2,400+ meals. The owner now displays a "Sharebite Partner" badge — and reports 15% more footfall from socially conscious customers.*

---

### 🚴 Story 3 — The Rider Who Changed His Neighbourhood

> *Rahul, a college student in Pune, started as a Sharebite rider part-time. In 3 months, he completed 180 deliveries, earning supplemental income and saving 2,700 meals from waste. He recruited 5 friends from his area. His neighborhood now has a 6-rider micro-fleet that handles all rescue deliveries within 5 km. Local NGOs call their area "the most food-secure zone in the city."*

---

### 🏥 Story 4 — The Hospital Kitchen

> *A large government hospital in Mumbai generates 50–80 kg of cooked food surplus daily from uneaten patient meals (safe, sealed, same-day). Previously, all of it went to waste. After partnering with Sharebite, the hospital lists food every evening. 2 NGOs now pick up the food regularly and distribute it to homeless shelters nearby. The hospital reduces its waste disposal cost and fulfils its CSR mandate simultaneously.*
